Show the current sample after a device reconnect

Brief for approval. Change C-0003-reconnect-sample, revision 1, 2026-11-12. Full brief, written by: independent reader.

What you get

  • While unplugged the display shows "No device", as today.
  • The return of the USB link empties the sample cache.
  • The display then shows no reading until the next device sample arrives, and shows that sample.
