Agree the wire representation of the sequence field

Brief for approval. Change C-0002-seq-wire, revision 1, 2026-11-12. Full brief, written by: independent reader.

What you get

  • The wire format does not move. The canonical definition already states the sequence field as a zigzag varint of at most ten bytes.
  • The consumer is corrected: it decodes that zigzag varint, not an unsigned varint.
  • The producer refuses to encode an integer outside -9223372036854775808 through 9223372036854775807.
  • The consumer refuses a varint longer than ten bytes or carrying more than 64 bits.
  • The values 0, -1, 1 and both range limits survive a round trip on both sides.
