Brief for approval. Change C-0001-kernel, revision 1, 2026-11-12. Full brief, written by: independent reader.

What you get

  • The method documents, schemas, four examples with briefs, one defective brief, the case list, a measurement proposal and the ledger become the standing method.
  • The grace5 CLI provides init, check, run, status and close as that method defines them.
  • Three skills (prepare, execute, review) exist once. The harness packages are generated from them.
  • A conformance suite proves every case in models/architecture/conformance.md.

Where it may reach

  • Nothing is copied from GRACE 4; its tests are read as examples only. The surface is exactly the method's list, with no deferred proposal. close refuses in the documented order and names the unmet obligation. Every mechanism has a ledger entry with a removal criterion. The kernel validates against the published XSD files, not a second copy of their rules.
